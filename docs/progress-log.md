## Round 5 (7 to 9 October 2026)

**9 October. Round 5 closes.** Both tracks are on `main` through pull request #20, which also carried the inference session's transcription of its closing memo. The root `README.md` now describes the project through round 5 and points to the reading guide, this log and `../CLAUDE.md`. The README's numeric-claim sweep found 17 claims it could not match, and all but one came from a checker fault under pandas 3 that made files with an empty text cell unreadable. With the fault fixed and the remaining value, the 0.1018 identity term of key finding 2, declared as a derived value, the sweep verifies 239 of 239 claims. Those two changes are on branch `oversight-checker-pandas3` for a pull request. The round's oversight steps moved out of `CLAUDE.md`, which keeps one line for the round. What comes next is open with Nicolas.

**9 October. The inference track closes** (`decisions/round5_ppi_E5_decisions.md`, report `round5_ppi_final_report.md`, pull request #20). The paper's estimator is defined once in `round5_ppi_estimator_definition.md`, with form C within clusters, the cross-fitted between coefficient, the interval with the cross-fitting term, and the stratified draw for the group difference. In regime B the predictors narrow the slope's interval to 0.706 to 0.847 of the classical width on lung and 0.594 to 0.625 on census, and not on the kidney tasks, 0.982 to 1.009 (`results/round5/ppi/E5_joint/e5_joint_design.csv`). Stage E4b added an interval for balanced selection of the labelled clusters, the classical mean with a residual variance. At 8 labelled clusters it is 0.726 of the simple-random classical width on kidney cancer with `uni_v2` and 0.551 on census by state, narrower than tuning under simple random selection at 0.852 and 0.618 (`results/round5/ppi/E4b_rejective/e4b_rej_width_ratios.csv`). With one balance variable and 8 or more labelled clusters it covers within 0.03 of the classical interval, and it fails with two balance variables at few residual degrees of freedom. Its null control failed in two census designs at 4 labelled clusters for a reason not established, so it enters the paper as a design result with conditions and not as the default. The allocation result uses the formula with the measured between ratio. The first question for a next round is weighting the labelled clusters by their inclusion probabilities under rejective selection.

**9 October. The prediction-set track closes** (`decisions/round5_conformal_W5_decisions.md`, report `round5_conf_final_report.md`, pull requests #18 and #19). On real data at 10 calibration donors and 90%, HCP with no labelled target spots is the narrowest valid method up to 5 labelled spots. From 9 labelled spots the full conformal set inside the target donor (`within_full`) is 0.416 to 0.677 of HCP's width and replaces round 4's recommendation of the plain split and the half split. On kidney cancer, GHCP at 5 labelled spots becomes narrower than HCP from 12 calibration donors, reaching 0.831 of HCP's width at 20, and holding the prediction head fixed shows that the number of calibration donors produces this. In GHCP's own simulation designs the full conformal set is 2 to 4 times narrower than GHCP from 9 labelled units wherever GHCP is finite. The released GHCP code calibrates HCP on half of the reference groups, so its 20-group design corresponds to 10 calibration clusters, which puts the paper's designs and the real-data map on one axis. On the theory side, two parts of the small-$K$ proposition are direct corollaries of Tibshirani, Barber and Ramdas, HCP is not pointwise dominated in the revealed-law model, and whether it is dominated in expected width is left open. The memo corrects a row count in the report (1,694,142 matched rows, not 1,693,662) and an error in the W2 memo ("3 to 4 times" should read 2 to 4 times).

**9 October. E4 gate of the inference track** (`decisions/round5_ppi_E4_decisions.md`, report `round5_ppi_E4_report.md`, pull request #17). Three things changed the paper's method.
- The design-target interval gains the cross-fitting term that the finite-population factor had removed. In simulation at 15 clusters and 12 labelled, the ratio of estimated to empirical variance goes from 0.69 to 0.91 without it to 0.97 to 1.01 with it. The paper's interval is `textbook_t|fpc|lin|xf` (`results/round5/ppi/E3a_crossfit/e3a_sim_prediction_cells.csv`).
- Form C, which gives each cluster its own intercept, becomes the paper's within-cluster estimator. A predictor constant within a cluster changes nothing under it. For the slope its classical interval is 4 to 18% narrower than the pooled-intercept form's (`results/round5/ppi/E3_twolevel/e3_prediction_cells.csv`).
- Drawing the group difference stratified by group inside each cluster repairs E2's under-coverage. It covers 0.885 to 0.913 at 6 or more labelled units per donor.

Balanced selection of the labelled clusters cuts the classical estimator's variance (to 0.604 of its value on kidney cancer for `uni_v2` and 0.264 on census by state, at 8 labelled clusters), but no interval run so far shows the gain. The memo adds E4b, an interval built on the classical mean with a residual variance, before the closing stage E5. Two errors were the oversight chat's. Its E2 memo misread three provenance records, and its band for the permuted-balance check assumed the genes gave independent replicates when every gene shares the same draws.

**8 October. W2 gate of the prediction-set track** (`decisions/round5_conformal_W2_decisions.md`, report `round5_conf_W2_report.md`, pull request #16). The simulation map was completed. The full conformal set inside the target cluster (`within_full`) is the narrowest method in 1,350 of 1,440 cells at a between-cluster share of 0.3 and in all 1,440 at 0.9 (`results/round5/conformal/W1_sim/w1_whole_grid_counts.csv`). In GHCP's fixed design with 20 groups, the released GHCP is 27.55 wide at 5 labelled units against 33.40 for HCP (`W2_ghcp_settings/w2_sim_designs.csv`). The memo also set the code-delivery rules both tracks now follow, which are one push per gate, commits delivered to Longleaf as bundles, and jobs run from read-only snapshots.

**8 October. E2 gate of the inference track** (`decisions/round5_ppi_E2_decisions.md`, report `round5_ppi_E2_report.md`, pull request #15). Three findings.
- In regime B (a few labelled units on every cluster) the round-4 classical comparator had no intercept, so a constant predictor appeared to help. Against a constant predictor, the encoders' width is 0.98 to 1.04 on the three kidney tasks, 0.74 to 0.84 on lung and 0.60 to 0.64 on census income (`results/round5/ppi/E2_regimeB/e2_decomposition.csv`). The constant predictor became the primary control.
- Simple random draws inside a donor miss rare groups, and the group difference then under-covers, down to 0.23 on lung at two units per donor (`e2_regimeB_grid.csv`).
- The tuned interval loses coverage when most clusters are labelled, 0.840 at 15 clusters and 12 labelled, because the cross-fitting term escapes the finite-population factor (`results/round5/ppi/E1_interval/e1_sim_grid.csv`).

Each finding produced a stage of interval 2: the regression form E3, the stratified draw, and the cross-fitting correction E3a.

**7 October. Round 5 starts as two tracks** (`decisions/round5_ppi_track.md`, `decisions/round5_conformal_track.md`, pull request #14). The second advisor meeting had stopped at slide 8, so the advisors had not yet seen round 4's results, and round 5 went ahead on the plan from the round-4 closing memos. The inference track would validate the final interval in simulation, complete regime B, put the estimators in regression form and choose which clusters to label. The prediction-set track would complete the simulation map, run GHCP in its own settings, extend the real-data map above 10 calibration donors and take up two theory questions.

## Before round 5

**28 September to 3 October. Round 4.** A data pull built the tasks (`round4_data_P8_report.md`, tag `round4-data-v2`), then two tracks ran. The inference track produced a valid interval with 4 to 12 labelled clusters, the gain theorem, the cost of tuning and the comparison of regimes A and B (closing page of `round4_ppi_final_report.md`). **25 to 28 September. The second change of direction.** PPI turned out to be the survey-sampling difference estimator and GHCP to occupy the labelled-test-group conformal setting, so the paper was reframed as "Labelling budgets for prediction-powered inference with clustered data" (`decisions/round4_originality_check.md`). Full account in `decisions/round4_oversight_handoff.md`.

**22 to 24 September. Round 3 and the first change of direction.** The first experiments for both original topics, and the retirement of Topics A and B as framed (`round3_final_report.md`, `decisions/round3_directions.md`).

**12 to 21 September. Rounds 1 and 2.** A faithful replication of the HEST-1k benchmark and its instrumentation. The `pca_ridge` head agrees with the live leaderboard to a mean absolute difference of 0.0002 over 108 encoder and task cells (`../README.md`, `results/summary/discrepancy_table.csv`). The first deck was presented through slide 6 of 14 on 21 September. Full account in `decisions/round3_oversight_handoff.md`.
