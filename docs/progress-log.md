**13 October, evening. The PPI paper's positioning** (`docs/round07/oversight/round07_ppi_positioning.md`). The oversight chat read Broska and Howes and "Balanced Active Inference" in full, and the cluster parts of Kluger et al., from PDFs Nicolas supplied. Broska and Howes's design formula, read at whole-cluster labelling, gives round 4's gain-theorem endpoint $1 - R^2_{\text{cluster}}\,G_U/(G_U + n_L)$ and its shrunken coefficient exactly, as the classical survey-sampling variance already did. So the paper cannot claim them as new, and the overlap check's list of what remains the paper's own is corrected. Their formula also shows that round 4's condition for regime B to win on the between-cluster term left out the unlabelled clusters' term, and with it regime B never loses on that term in the superpopulation. Broska and Howes have no small-sample correction and cover 0.88 at about 113 labelled clusters, and they leave costs and the choice of clusters to future work. Kluger et al. already weight labelled clusters by known probabilities under independent Bernoulli selection, so round 7's open question narrows to fixed-size rejective selection with dependent inclusions at 4 to 16 clusters. Chen et al.'s remark 1 says balancing on the predictions forfeits the benefit of active sampling, while round 5 found that selecting clusters on their predictions and using the classical mean beat tuned PPI at 8 labelled clusters. The note recommends accepting O2 with three changes, and the paper's own ground becomes the few-cluster, design-target regime and the design questions. An independent agent re-derived the central formula and found 14 other problems, all fixed before commit.
