**26 October, track fewcluster, evening. The frontier, proved twice.** `docs/round09/i01/fewcluster/round09_fewcluster_frontier.md` states and proves the max-share frontier. Under simple random sampling of $n$ of $G$ clusters, a location and scale equivariant interval can be valid in finite samples over every population whose residual max-share is at most $\kappa_0$ when $\kappa_0 < \kappa_2(k^\ast - 1)$, and above it every valid equivariant interval is unbounded with positive probability under every population. The two-point population is extremal, and no fixed-size design blind to the outcomes does better than simple random sampling. A fresh agent proved every statement again from the statements alone (`results/round9/fewcluster/verify/`). **9 October. The prediction-set track closes** (`docs/round05/i03/conformal/round5_conformal_W5_decisions.md`, report `round5_conf_final_report.md`, pull requests #18 and #19). On real data at 10 calibration donors and 90%, HCP with no labelled target spots is the narrowest valid method up to 5 labelled spots. From 9 labelled spots the full conformal set inside the target donor (`within_full`) is 0.416 to 0.677 of HCP's width and replaces round 4's recommendation of the plain split and the half split. On kidney cancer, GHCP at 5 labelled spots becomes narrower than HCP from 12 calibration donors, reaching 0.831 of HCP's width at 20, and holding the prediction head fixed shows that the number of calibration donors produces this. In GHCP's own simulation designs the full conformal set is 2 to 4 times narrower than GHCP from 9 labelled units wherever GHCP is finite. The released GHCP code calibrates HCP on half of the reference groups, so its 20-group design corresponds to 10 calibration clusters, which puts the paper's designs and the real-data map on one axis. On the theory side, two parts of the small-$K$ proposition are direct corollaries of Tibshirani, Barber and Ramdas, HCP is not pointwise dominated in the revealed-law model, and whether it is dominated in expected width is left open. **9 October. E4 gate of the inference track** (`docs/round05/i03/ppi/round5_ppi_E4_decisions.md`, report `round5_ppi_E4_report.md`, pull request #17). Three things changed the paper's method.
- The design-target interval gains the cross-fitting term that the finite-population factor had removed. In simulation at 15 clusters and 12 labelled, the ratio of estimated to empirical variance goes from 0.69 to 0.91 without it to 0.97 to 1.01 with it. The paper's interval is `textbook_t|fpc|lin|xf` (`results/round5/ppi/E3a_crossfit/e3a_sim_prediction_cells.csv`).
- Form C, which gives each cluster its own intercept, becomes the paper's within-cluster estimator. A predictor constant within a cluster changes nothing under it. For the slope its classical interval is 4 to 18% narrower than the pooled-intercept form's (`results/round5/ppi/E3_twolevel/e3_prediction_cells.csv`).
- Drawing the group difference stratified by group inside each cluster repairs E2's under-coverage. It covers 0.885 to 0.913 at 6 or more labelled units per donor.

Balanced selection of the labelled clusters cuts the classical estimator's variance (to 0.604 of its value on kidney cancer for `uni_v2` and 0.264 on census by state, at 8 labelled clusters), but no interval run so far shows the gain. The memo adds E4b, an interval built on the classical mean with a residual variance, before the closing stage E5. Two errors were the oversight chat's. Its E2 memo misread three provenance records, and its band for the permuted-balance check assumed the genes gave independent replicates when every gene shares the same draws.

**8 October. E2 gate of the inference track** (`docs/round05/i02/ppi/round5_ppi_E2_decisions.md`, report `round5_ppi_E2_report.md`, pull request #15). Three findings.
- In regime B (a few labelled units on every cluster) the round-4 classical comparator had no intercept, so a constant predictor appeared to help. Against a constant predictor, the encoders' width is 0.98 to 1.04 on the three kidney tasks, 0.74 to 0.84 on lung and 0.60 to 0.64 on census income (`results/round5/ppi/E2_regimeB/e2_decomposition.csv`). Each finding produced a stage of interval 2: the regression form E3, the stratified draw, and the cross-fitting correction E3a.
