## 1. What Nicolas said

On 6 November 2026, after the migration of the record and before any new direction had been given, Nicolas wrote this in the oversight chat. The elision is his own, marking a part of his message about the survey he asked for.

> The aim should never have been doing everything we can to write a "novel" method/theory paper by filling in whatever gap we can find in the literature, merely for the sake of coming up with new method or theory. What motivated this project initially was quantifying uncertainty in histology to expression prediction and conducting valid inference using predicted expression, since prediction accuracy has stalled. It is perfectly acceptable, as a first paper, to apply existing/classical methods and theory to our specific context and data, to answer the questions we posed. New method and theory should arise naturally as we encounter true problems in practice during the process, and should never be the main goal. [...] The importance of literature search remains from before. Whenever we run into a problem, always search the literature first for existing methods and theory to apply before any analysis. Only when a genuine gap exists should we begin devising our own method and deriving our own theory. Delegate tasks that can be run in parallel to opus subagents.

The oversight chat reads this as four standing points and one instruction. The aim is to answer the questions the project posed, and applying existing or classical methods and theory to the project's context and data is acceptable for a first paper. New method and theory arise from problems met in practice and are never the main goal. Whenever the work meets a problem, the literature is searched first for the existing method or theory to apply, before any analysis, and the project's own method is devised only where that search finds a genuine gap. And tasks that can run in parallel are delegated to Opus subagents, which the record now carries as a working rule until Nicolas narrows it.

## 2. What this changes, as the oversight chat reads it `[proposed]`

## 3. How the record reads under the reframing `[proposed]`

Three things follow from the survey.

First, the project is much closer to a paper than the methods-first framing made it look. Read against the original questions, rounds 3 to 9 answered most of both with existing methods, on three audited tasks and a public dataset (`round10_findings_survey.md`, sections 2.2 to 2.4). The results that rounds 7 and 8 counted as losses, because a published paper or a textbook already held them, are exactly the citations an applied paper needs.

Second, the one piece of theory that came from a problem met in practice is round 9's frontier. The project saw under-coverage with few labelled donors from round 4 on, round 5 traced it to skewed donor contributions, and the frontier says when a finite-sample guarantee is possible at all and how many clusters it takes (B8, B11, B13 in the survey). Under the reframing it belongs in the paper as the explanation of an observed failure and the basis of the design advice, not as the paper's reason to exist, and its novelty stays provisional until the sources are read. Theorem 2, the critical values and the median result were sought for the paper's shape rather than for a problem on the data, and the paper does not need them (survey D, section 7(f)).

Third, the inference numbers the paper would report are not yet the final ones. Rounds 5 to 7 reported tuned intervals computed with round 5's estimator, which round 8 found to be Williams' without one factor, and with a variance that round 9, rerunning round 5's code on the tasks, found to grow with the level of the predictions, widening its intervals by 1.09 to 1.53 times on HEST and 9 to 20 times on ACS and lifting coverage by 0.025 to 0.046 (B9, B10). Whether the intervals reported in rounds 5 to 7 carry that inflation has not been checked. Under the reframing this is a problem met in practice with an existing solution. An exactly unbiased form is in print (Williams 1961; the fold-wise form of Lu et al. 2025 and An et al. 2026), a fixed coefficient of 1 does as well as any tuned form at these sizes (B10), and the literature may already hold the variance estimator round 9 listed as missing (Sanguiao Sande and Zhang 2020 give an unbiased variance estimator for the single-split form, and Mickey 1959 is unread). The analyses are rerun once with the cited estimator before anything is written.

## 4. What the paper still lacks `[proposed]`

1. **A final set of inference results with the corrected, cited estimator** on the three HEST tasks and ACS, in both regimes, with the design comparison (simple random selection, `rej_t`, the cube design, the published comparators) redone on the same footing, and with the check of whether rounds 5 to 7's reported intervals carried the inflation. This is the main computational task and the only one that needs Longleaf. It also settles which CCRCC donor set the paper uses, the 24 donors or the 23-donor merged set, both of which run through the record.
2. **A decision on the estimands' normalization** (D5). Every estimand of rounds 3 to 9 is on $\log(1 + y)$ of unnormalized counts, which partly measures sequencing depth (D11). An applied paper has to say what expression it makes inference about, and the literature-first rule applies here too, since the spatial transcriptomics literature has conventions for it. This needs David.
3. **An answer to whether regime B can be bought on a spatial platform** (Q1), and a ruling on the variance-share bound (V) that regime B's few-cluster numbers rest on (E1). On Visium a donor is assayed whole, so "a few spots in every donor" is not a purchase a lab can make as it stands, and the paper's cost model has to say what it means (a cheaper or lower-plex assay on every donor, or a design result stated for domains where units are purchasable, such as the census). Q1 has not been asked of David.
4. **The methods ledger.** One document that lists, for each question the paper answers, the existing method applied, its source with read level, where it is implemented, what was validated, and where a genuine gap remains. Most of its rows exist across the checks of rounds 4, 7, 8 and 9 and need consolidating; the missing rows are the ones the reframing's rule now requires before the final runs.
5. **The sources an applied paper must cite, read where they have not been.** Round 8 read Williams (1961) in full and Sanguiao Sande and Zhang (2020) in full, Yang, Qu and Li in their arXiv version, and HCP and GHCP in part. Still unread are the textbook chapters behind the regime and allocation results (Cochran 1977, chapters 5, 7 and 9 to 11; Särndal, Swensson and Wretman 1992, chapter 8), Mickey (1959), the published statement of Yang, Qu and Li (JASA 2023), and GHCP's full text, which round 8 asked to be checked for a comparison with the target donor's own full conformal set before the paper claims the prediction-set map (`docs/round08/i01/positioning/round08_positioning_note.md`, sections 3.10 and 7). Williams' variance estimator (his equation 13) and Sanguiao Sande and Zhang's unbiased variance estimator need a second reading with the final estimator in mind. Round 8 listed ten sources with UNC library links.
6. **The prediction-set results on the same footing as the inference results**, rerun on the final prediction files with INT5 and the cross-gene set included (A6, A10), since the current map was built in round 5 on round 3's base predictor and has no standard errors on its width margins, and the adaptive-score result (A5) ran at reduced gene scope (CQR on 6 of 50 genes, the negative binomial head on 16).
7. **A ruling on the joint design** (E8), which R8-1 and the reconciliation with the direction scan's JOINT verdict leave open.
8. **The advisors' view.** They have seen the project up to slide 8 of the second deck on 7 October, before any result of rounds 4 to 9 (`CLAUDE.md`, "What the advisors have seen").

## 6. Round 10 as proposed `[proposed]`

**Goal.** The applied paper of the survey's section 4.2, drafted by January 2027 and submitted by spring 2027, with every method cited to its source and the project's own method only where the ledger records a genuine gap.

**Tracks, in order.**

1. **`ledger`** (by whoever decision 13 names, with Opus subagents in parallel, one per section of the paper outline, no compute). Build the methods ledger of section 4 item 4 and the list of genuine gaps, read the sources of decision 3 if that decision lifts the no-reads rule, and write the writing plan. Its gate is Nicolas's review of the ledger. It also hands the final-runs track its brief, with predictions written first. About one week.
2. **`final-runs`** (one execution session under `docs/EXECUTION_RULES.md`, placed as decision 13 says, with every job on Longleaf). One clean set of analyses with the cited methods. The inference results in both regimes with the exactly unbiased estimator, the fixed and cross-fitted coefficients and a level-free variance, including the check of whether rounds 5 to 7's intervals carried the inflation. The design comparison redone on that footing. The prediction-set map rerun with INT5 and the cross-gene set, and the adaptive scores at full gene scope. The probe and take-all design. The frontier numbers per task re-cited. Gates at anchors (one round 5 cell and one round 7 cell reproduced), at the inference results, at the prediction sets and designs, and at the final report with its pull request. About three weeks.
3. **`paper`** (the oversight chat). Draft v1 of the applied paper from the writing plan and the final report, with a fresh-agent check, then the advisor deck. This track does not start until final-runs' final gate.

**Predictions to write into the masterplan before any run** (candidates). The corrected variance lowers rounds 5 to 7's tuned coverage on tissue by 0.02 to 0.05 and changes no design ranking. The ledger finds a published variance estimator for the exactly unbiased split-sample form and no published finite-sample valid interval at the real tasks' sizes. The prediction-set map's switch from HCP to the target donor's own full conformal set stays at 9 labelled spots on CCRCC and LUNG_XENIUM, and on INDIANA_KIDNEY the plain split stays within 2% of it at 9 to 15 labelled spots, as round 5 found.

**What is not in round 10.** No search for a methodological core. No new theory unless a problem in the final runs has no existing solution after a documented search, in which case the session stops at a gate and reports the gap rather than deriving. No further data download. No contact outside the project.

**How the literature-first rule runs inside the round.** Every brief carries, for each analysis, the method and its source from the ledger. When a session meets a problem the ledger does not cover, it searches first with subagents whose only task is to find the existing method, records the search in its results directory, and reports at its next gate. A derivation starts only after Nicolas has seen the search and agreed the gap is genuine. This is the gate of decision 11, and it narrows the autonomy rule of 24 October for this round.
