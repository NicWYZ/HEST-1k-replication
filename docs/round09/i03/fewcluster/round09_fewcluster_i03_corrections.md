# Round 9, track fewcluster, corrections to the interval 3 record

*27 October 2026. Written by the fewcluster session after an automated review of the interval's work. Committed reports are not edited, so the corrections are listed here. None changes a theorem, a verdict or a headline number.*

1. **Pötscher and Preinerstorfer's Theorem 5.1.** `round09_fewcluster_critical_value.md`, section 2, item 1, says a size-controlling critical value exists "if and only if" no extreme-leverage configuration exists. The theorem as displayed gives sufficient conditions, which their discussion says are necessary only in some cases. The same description should replace "a yes-or-no condition of the same shape" in section 1 of `docs/round09/tracks/fewcluster/paper_draft_v0.md` when the draft is revised.
2. **Certificates near the frontier.** Section 4 of the paper draft says no certificate was found near the frontier. At 12 of 24 and 0.9 of the frontier the band-lemma certificate gives $c = 196.1$. That value is valid but useless. At 0.9 of the frontier the other three designs have no certificate (`results/round9/fewcluster/crit/part1_table.csv`).
3. **A wrong DOI in NOV10's candidates.** `results/round9/fewcluster/novelty/novelty_NOV10_STRAT_candidates.csv` gives 10.3982/ECTA14240 for Canay, Romano and Shaikh (2017), which Crossref does not know. Crossref gives 10.3982/ecta13081 for "Randomization Tests Under an Approximate Symmetry Assumption", Econometrica 2017.
4. **NOV11's query log.** In `novelty_NOV11_CRIT_queries.csv` the 15 web-search rows are shortened reconstructions of the queries, not the verbatim strings. The per-source counts in the checker's deviations sum to 44, while the file has 45 rows (20 arXiv rows, not 19).
5. **Theorem 3 verifier report.** `results/round9/fewcluster/verify_strat/verify_strat_report.md` says the monotonicity of $\pi$ and the bound $R \le \pi(\lfloor N/2 \rfloor)$ were checked for $N \le 40$. For odd $N$ above 10 only domination of the $R$ level by some two-point level was checked. The report also says the defeating populations have coverage exactly $1 - \prod \pi$ for $c$ up to $10^6$ at jitter $10^{-6}$. Its output shows coverage returning toward 1 once $c$ reaches about the reciprocal of the jitter, as it must. Neither affects a conclusion, since the dynamic programme and the 2216 single-stratum cases agree.
6. **Critical-value verifier report.** `results/round9/fewcluster/verify_crit/verify_crit_report.md` counts five spot-checked lower bounds in its verdict table and tabulates four.
7. **Box-class script.** The docstring of `code/scripts/round9_fewcluster_box.py` named an `acs` mode that exited with an error. The working mode is `acs_states`, and the docstring and code are corrected in the commit that adds this note. The numbers in `results/round9/fewcluster/strat/` were produced with `acs_states` and `hest` and are unchanged (rerun gives the same smallest $m$, 2 and 5).
