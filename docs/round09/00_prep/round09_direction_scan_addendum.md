# Round 9 preparation, addendum to the direction scan

*25 October 2026. The claude-science session. It adds the one verdict that was pending when `round09_direction_scan.md` was committed. That document is not edited.*

The novelty checker HCPWIDTH was parked on an approval card until Nicolas approved it, and then finished. Its claim was a finite-sample width law for hierarchical conformal sets as a function of the number of calibration donors K, the labelled spots o in the target donor, alpha and the between and within decomposition of the score law, with a switching point in o and an optimal combination rule. Its verdict is partly open (`results/round9/scan/scan_HCPWIDTH_queries.csv`, `results/round9/scan/scan_HCPWIDTH_candidates.csv`, and the entry HCPWIDTH in `results/round9/scan/scan_structured_results.json`).

GHCP (Mallick, Tchetgen Tchetgen, Dobriban and Lee, arXiv:2608.15500, read in full by the checker) occupies the setting of K reference donors and a target donor with o labelled units. It compares GHCP, HCP, the target-only set and Dunn, Wasserman and Ramdas's sets by simulation, and lists efficiency and optimal data-adaptive mergers as open. The single-group machinery for expected size is Dhillon, Deligiannidis and Rainforth (arXiv:2306.07254). Duchi, Gupta, Jiang and Sur (arXiv:2403.16336) and Zhang, Li, Xie, Kong and Jiang (JMLR 2026) treat borrowing against own data only asymptotically. Not found in any retrieved paper were an exact or closed-form width law for these sets in K and o, the switching point o* at which the target-only set becomes narrower, an optimal merger rule with finite-sample validity, and a lower bound on width under hierarchical exchangeability with o target labels. The checker judged a width formula alone too thin, because it applies Dhillon et al.'s order-statistic integral to HCP's weighted empirical law, and named the defensible version as a lower bound or exact crossover together with a finite-sample valid optimal merger. It also noted the scoop risk from the GHCP group.

This is the most specific open remainder the scan found, and it bears on option C of the scan's section 4 (the prediction-set half as the centre). The project's round 5 map, with the switch from HCP to the target donor's own set at 9 labelled spots (`docs/round07/oversight/round07_ppi_results_explained.md`, A3), is an empirical instance of the crossover o*.
