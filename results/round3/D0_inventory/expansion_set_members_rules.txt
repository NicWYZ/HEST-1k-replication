## institution_breast_xenium (18 samples, 22.903 GB over the four components, 4 in the benchmark)

Selection rule. Every sample in the v1_3_0 inventory with species Homo sapiens, organ Breast,
st_technology exactly Xenium, and oncotree_code IDC, after removing flagged duplicates
(is_duplicate_of_other_id True). No other filter is applied: disease state, preservation method,
laboratory and pixel size are left as they are, because this set's purpose is the two-source
contrast with disease, platform and preservation fixed and the sources are established in D3.

What the filter removes, counted. 140 human Breast samples in the release; 21 of them are Xenium
(108 Spatial Transcriptomics, 8 Visium, 3 Visium HD); of the 21 Xenium, 18 are IDC, 2 are ILC and
1 has no oncotree code. st_technology is matched exactly, so Xenium 5k is not included. None of
the 18 carries a duplicate flag, so the duplicate removal is a no-op on this set and the count is
18 before and after.

The 18 ids. NCBI783;NCBI784;NCBI785;TENX191;TENX192;TENX193;TENX195;TENX196;TENX197;TENX198;
TENX199;TENX200;TENX201;TENX202;TENX95;TENX97;TENX98;TENX99

Overlap. NCBI783, NCBI784 and NCBI785 are also members of platform_pair_multitech, which is why
the union of the three round-3 sets is 105 samples and not 108. The four already in the benchmark
(task IDC) are NCBI783, NCBI785, TENX95 and TENX99.

## kidney_visium_cell_extras (4 samples, 1.526 GB over the four components, 0 in the benchmark)

These are the four samples that are in the Kidney-on-Visium human cell of the release but outside
D0's institution_kidney_visium set. The cell holds 58 non-duplicate human samples; the institution
set holds 54. They are downloaded in D1 and are kept out of every analysis set definition; D3
audits them. The reason each is excluded from the analysis set:

NCBI538  disease_state Treated. Subseries "PFA fixed kidney_organoid_3", cohort "Genome-wide
         Spatial Expression Profiling in Formalin-fixed tissues", lab_provisional KTH Royal
         Institute of Technology. Treated here means PFA-fixed material and the subseries names
         it organoid, so it may not be donor tissue at all. D0's institution rule excludes an
         institution whose samples in the cell are all disease_state Treated, which is what
         removed KTH.
NCBI539  disease_state Treated. Same cohort and laboratory, subseries "PFA fixed
         kidney_organoid_2". Same reason.
NCBI540  disease_state Treated. Same cohort and laboratory, subseries "PFA fixed
         kidney_organoid_1". Same reason.
TENX71   lab_basis vendor_product_page. Cohort "Human Kidney, 11 mm Capture Area (FFPE)",
         lab_provisional "10x Genomics Inc. (vendor product page)". A vendor product page is a
         download source, not a generating laboratory, so this sample cannot carry an institution
         label and D0's rule excludes vendor product pages.
