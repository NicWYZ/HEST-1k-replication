What is in this directory
-------------------------
superseded_tag_manifest.csv
    One row per committed round-3 CSV that carries an IDC `audited` row, with its row count, the
    number of rows tagged, and the sha256 before and after the tag was added. 229 files, 14,149
    rows tagged True over 178,150 rows. Scope was fixed by rule, not by hand: a CSV is in scope
    when it has both a `task` and a `label_set` column and at least one row with task == IDC and
    label_set == audited. 352 CSVs exist under results/round3/; 229 are in scope.

a3_report_numbers_rerun_comparison.csv
    The proof that the tag moved nothing numeric. results/round3/A3_report_numbers.csv was copied
    before the tagging run, round3_a3_report_numbers.py was rerun afterwards over the tagged
    tables, and the two files are byte-identical (388 rows, sha256 in the file, max absolute
    difference over the value column 0.0). The A1 anchor inside that table,
    a2b_anchor_max_abs_diff = 7.771561172e-16, is therefore reproduced unchanged.

h1_numeric_gate_summary.csv
    Per-document result of the numeric-claim gate, in the two invocations above, over every
    document H1 edited and every round-3 document. 0 not found and 0 uncited everywhere.

h1_override_guard_fixture.csv
    Both-directions fixture for the builder's override guards, run in a /tmp fixture tree so the
    clone was not perturbed: 12 cases, 3 accepted and 9 refused. The three accepted are the
    committed overrides file and an override on each of the two owned columns this stage did not
    use (donor_label_status, notes); the nine refused are donor_id, row_origin, sample_id, two
    columns that exist but are outside the three the track owns (disease, hest_patient), a column
    that does not exist, a sample_id matching no row, an empty citation, and a duplicate key.

h1_numeric_gate_round3_and_readme.tsv, h1_numeric_gate_h1_docs.tsv
    The gate's own --tsv output for the same two invocations. Both are header-only, which is the
    record that no claim was left unresolved.

tracked_round3_csv_at_a181326.txt
    Written by the lead session, not by this track: the output of `git ls-files` over
    results/round3/*.csv at a181326, supplied so this track could confirm that the 229 files it
    tagged are all tracked and that nothing untracked was touched. Reconciled: 349 tracked, 349
    CSVs on disk before this stage, all 229 tagged files in the tracked set.
