MAIN RULE: all 50 genes rise and fall together with how much cellular tissue is in the spot. Sections differ about 3-fold in overall level, and patches within a section differ about 2-fold.
- Dense viable tumour nests full of nuclei: highest (typical genes 0.5-0.8, LY6E/IFI27 about 1.5, CPE/HTRA1 about 1.1).
- Hypocellular collagen, hyalinised scar, oedema, lumen/empty space, pure blood, necrosis: everything about half (most genes 0.15-0.3, IFI27/LY6E about 0.7, LY6E near 0.2 in blood, about 0 in pure fibrin/necrosis).
- Lymphoid aggregates: medium overall, but TRBC1, CD14, S100A9, H3F3A, TFRC and AKAP8 are relatively high.
- Vessel walls, haemorrhage edges and congested capillaries: EGFL7, HMOX1, CD14, PARP10 and S100A9 rise, while LY6E, CIB1 and LRRC41 drop.

A. High-grade solid tumour: large pleomorphic vesicular nuclei with nucleoli, eosinophilic or amphophilic cytoplasm, giant cells, patchy necrosis and haemorrhage. Highest level overall. CPE about 3.3 (often 3-5), CD109 1.5, CIB1 1.3, CKB 1.2, SEMA4B 1.2, LY6E 1.9, IFI27 1.6, FLNB 1.4, LRRC41 1.3, DERL1 0.9, IFI6 0.9.

Highest myeloid and interferon signal: IFI27 2.2, IFI6 1.75, LY6E 1.7, S100A9 1.7, CD14 1.6, TRBC1 1.6, NT5DC2 1.4, TFRC 1.4, FLNB 1.1, ISG15 1.0, LAPTM4B 1.0, GPNMB 0.9, CPE only 0.45.

D. Spindled atypical tumour cells in collagenous or fasciculated stroma (sarcomatoid-like): HTRA1 very high (2.7), LY6E 1.5, NOTCH3 0.7, LAPTM4B 0.7, CPE 0.9; CD109, CKB, S100A9, GPNMB and TRBC1 low (about 0.2).
E. Clear-cell tumour with lymphocyte-rich areas and a fibrous capsule: medium level, LY6E 1.2, HSPA1A 1.0, LRRC41 0.9, IFI27 0.9, TMEM91 0.7; most other genes 0.2-0.6.
F. Clear-cell tumour with lots of haemorrhage, oedema, hyalinised stroma: low level, with most genes 0.1-0.4, but HSPA1A 0.9, LRRC41 0.8, IFI27 0.6.
G. Mostly fibrotic or hyalinised tissue with only scattered tumour, lymphoid clusters and haemosiderin: lowest level, with most genes 0.1-0.3, IFI6 0.75, HTRA1 0.6, GADD45B 0.6, HSPA1A 0.55.

GENE GROUPS (shifts within a section are modest; correlation per patch is only about 0.1-0.2):
- Tumour-cell/abundance: LY6E, LRRC41, CIB1, SEMA4B, CD109, CKB, CPE, FLNB, DERL1, SLC15A4, TMEM91, HNRNPK. Highest in dense nests and lowest in stroma and blood.
- Interferon: IFI27, IFI6, ISG15, IRF7, PARP10 and LY6E co-vary. They are high where tumour is inflamed.
- Myeloid: CD14, S100A9, GPNMB and HMOX1, plus TRBC1 for T cells. These rise with inflammatory infiltrate, neutrophils, macrophages, haemorrhage and debris.
- Stroma/vascular: HTRA1 and NOTCH3 (pericytes, smooth muscle, fibroblasts), and EGFL7 (endothelium). These are relatively spared in stroma compared with the other genes.
- Stress: HSPA1A and GADD45B are fairly flat, moderate even in low sections.
Use the section-type level, scaled by cellularity: a tumour-dense patch is about 1.3x the section average, a stroma/blood/empty patch about 0.6x.
