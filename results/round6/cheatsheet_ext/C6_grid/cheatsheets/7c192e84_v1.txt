Main rule: section-wide tumour type sets levels more than local detail. All genes rise and fall together with cell density (a depth effect): dense tumour-cell sheets ~1.3x mean; hyalinised collagen, oedematous or sparse stroma, empty space ~0.5x. Within a section, image cues are weak; predict close to the type level.
