CHEATSHEET: predicting 50 genes from H&E patches of clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC)
------------------------------

Data basis: Visium spots (55 um) from 7 ccRCC sections, 500 spots each. Each patch is 224x224 px
at 0.5 um/px (about 112 um across, so the spot is the central half of the patch). Values are
log(1+count). Counts are LOW: for a typical gene 50-70% of spots are 0, and nonzero values are
mostly 0.69 (1 count), 1.10 (2), 1.39 (3). So any single spot is noisy, and the best guess for a
patch is a moderate mean value (often 0.2-1.5), never exact integers.

THE BIG PICTURE (read first)
1. The largest differences are BETWEEN TUMOURS (sections), not between patches of one section.
   The total signal per spot differed about 3-fold between sections (lowest in sections that are
   fibrotic or bleeding with small bland clear cells; highest in high-grade, cell-dense,
   inflamed tumours). So first decide which TUMOUR TYPE the patch shows (section-level
   pattern, classes A-F below), then adjust for what the patch contains.
2. Within one section, almost every gene goes up and down together with how cell-rich and
   tumour-rich the patch is. Cell-dense tumour patches have the most counts; acellular
   collagen, hyalinised stroma, smooth muscle bundles, oedema/fluid, empty space and pure
   blood have the fewest (roughly 0.3-0.8x the tumour level of the same section).
3. Stain colour differs strongly between sections and is NOT biology: some sections are
   blue-purple (haematoxylin-heavy), others intensely pink-magenta, with nuclei hard to see.
   Both low-expression and high-expression tumours occurred in each colour family. Ignore
   overall hue; judge nuclear size, cytoplasm, architecture, cellularity and inflammation.

SECTION-LEVEL TUMOUR TYPES SEEN (decide which the new patch most resembles)
A. Classic low-grade clear-cell carcinoma. Sheets/nests of cells with water-clear or wispy
   pale cytoplasm, small round dark nuclei (no visible nucleoli), delicate capillary network
   ("chicken-wire"), often scattered lymphocytes; at the edges fibrous capsule, haemorrhage
   with packed red cells, haemosiderin (golden-brown/black pigment in macrophages), oedema
   and hyalinised pink stroma. Lowish overall expression. Relatively high: HSPA1A (~0.95),
   LRRC41 (~0.8), IFI27/LY6E (~0.75), TMEM91 (~0.5); low: NT5DC2 (~0.1), IRF7, S100A9, CKB,
   CD109 (~0.15). If the clear cells are crowded and lymphocytes are plentiful, LY6E (~1.2),
   TMEM91 (~0.75), CIB1 (~0.65), DERL1 (~0.6) are higher; if haemorrhage, pigment and
   hyalinised/oedematous stroma dominate, everything is lower (total about 2/3).
B. Fibrotic, poorly cellular renal tissue: dense pink collagen and smooth-muscle-like
   bundles, thick-walled vessels, scattered atrophic or compressed tubules (some with
   eosinophilic casts), a few lymphocytes, very few clear tumour cells. LOWEST expression of
   all: most genes 0.1-0.3. Relatively kept: CPE ~0.55, HTRA1 ~0.6, GADD45B ~0.55, IFI6 ~0.75,
   IFI27 ~0.55, HSPA1A ~0.55, RXRA ~0.4, NOTCH3 ~0.35, PTGS1 ~0.3. Very low: LRRC41 ~0.2,
   LY6E ~0.25, TMEM91, NOL3, SCARB1, YWHAE, BTF3, IRF7 (~0.1).
C. Clear-cell carcinoma with an alveolar/acinar pattern: cells with distinct cell borders,
   clear-to-finely-vacuolated cytoplasm, nests separated by thin fibrovascular septa with
   capillaries, some solid areas of smaller crowded cells. Medium-high expression with a
   distinctive signature: SLC39A14 very high (~2.2, often 2-3 in tumour), LRRC41 high (~1.6),
   IFI27 ~1.8, CPE ~1.2, HTRA1 ~1.1, PXN ~0.85, TMEM91 ~0.75, SLC15A4 ~0.75, NOTCH3 ~0.7,
   PTGS1 ~0.7, SCARB1 ~0.7, NOL3 ~0.65, AKAP8 ~0.6, TP53INP1 ~0.45, EGFL7 ~0.45, TRBC1 ~0.75;
   low S100A9 (~0.15), CKB, NT5DC2, GPNMB.
   (SLC39A14, SCARB1, NOL3, TMEM91, LRRC41, PTGS1 form a group that is high in vascular,
   lipid-rich, well-differentiated clear-cell tumour.)
D. High-grade eosinophilic/granular tumour, cell-dense, discohesive, with clefts and many
   inflammatory cells (neutrophils, macrophages, lymphocytes) mixed between tumour cells,
   little stroma. HIGHEST total expression. Myeloid/inflammatory and interferon genes high:
   S100A9 ~1.7, CD14 ~1.6, TRBC1 ~1.6, IFI27 ~2.2, IFI6 ~1.75, LY6E ~1.75, ISG15 ~1.0, IRF7
   ~0.65, PARP10 ~0.8, GPNMB ~0.85; proliferation/iron/lysosome genes high: NT5DC2 ~1.4, TFRC
   ~1.35, LAPTM4B ~1.05, FLNB ~1.1, GADD45B ~0.9, HNRNPK ~0.85, DNAJB12 ~0.6, RXRA ~0.75,
   SCARB1 ~0.7, PXN ~0.9, CIB1 ~0.9, NAMPT ~0.8, VMP1 ~0.9. Low: CKB ~0.1, LRRC41 ~0.4,
   TMEM91 ~0.3, CPE ~0.45.
   Within such tumour, the most loosely cohesive patches full of small round inflammatory
   cells and debris have the highest S100A9 (often 2-3.5) and CD14; compact, fibrous patches
   have S100A9 near 0.
E. Sarcomatoid/spindle-cell or desmoplastic tumour: plump spindle and epithelioid tumour
   cells with eosinophilic cytoplasm and vesicular nuclei, set in abundant collagenous,
   fibroblast-rich stroma; few clear cells; small lymphoid clusters. Hallmark: HTRA1 very
   high (~2.7; often 2-5) because HTRA1 is a fibroblast/matrix gene; NOTCH3 ~0.7 (pericytes,
   myofibroblasts), LY6E ~1.5, LAPTM4B ~0.7, YWHAE ~0.45, BTF3 ~0.45. Low: TRBC1 ~0.2,
   S100A9 ~0.2, CD14 ~0.3, LRRC41 ~0.25, SLC39A14 ~0.4, CD109 ~0.25, CKB ~0.15.
F. High-grade nested tumour with large nuclei and prominent nucleoli, abundant pale-
   amphophilic to granular cytoplasm (only partly clear), cell borders visible, thin
   fibrovascular septa, foci of haemorrhage. High total expression with a tumour-cell
   signature: CPE very high (~3.3; 4-5.5 in dense tumour), CD109 ~1.5, CKB ~1.2, SEMA4B ~1.2,
   FLNB ~1.4, CIB1 ~1.3, LRRC41 ~1.35, LY6E ~1.9, IFI27 ~1.6, CHST15 ~0.9, DERL1 ~0.9,
   PXN ~1.0, TFRC ~0.7, TMEM91 ~0.7, SLC15A4 ~0.75, KRTCAP2 ~0.5. Low: EGFL7 ~0.17,
   NOTCH3 ~0.3, SLC39A14 ~0.4, PTGS1 ~0.25, HTRA1 ~0.75.
   (CPE, CKB, CD109, SEMA4B rise and fall together; they mark this high-grade tumour-cell
   state and are low in classic small-nucleus clear cells and in stroma.)
If unsure which type: use the overall means above, nudged toward the closest type.
Grade cue: larger nuclei with nucleoli and granular/eosinophilic cytoplasm -> expect higher
totals and higher interferon (IFI27, IFI6, ISG15, LY6E), FLNB, PXN, TFRC, CIB1, CHST15;
small dark nuclei in water-clear cells -> lower, with HSPA1A and LRRC41 relatively high.

PATCH-LEVEL ADJUSTMENTS WITHIN A SECTION (relative to that section's tumour level)
- Cell-dense tumour (nuclei close together, little stroma): highest; add ~10-25% to the
  section mean for most genes, especially IFI27, LY6E, LRRC41, NOL3, SLC39A14, DERL1, CPE.
- Mixed tumour + stroma (about half each): about 0.75-0.85x; drops most in CPE, LY6E, LRRC41,
  HTRA1 (unless the stroma is cellular fibroblastic, see E), NAMPT, NOL3, IFI6.
- Pure fibrous/hyalinised stroma, capsule, vessel wall, smooth muscle: 0.3-0.8x tumour;
  the most acellular pale-pink collagen is near 0 for most genes. Relatively spared in
  stroma: NOTCH3, EGFL7 (vessel walls, pericytes), LAPTM4B, NT5DC2, ISG15, TP53INP1, BTF3.
  Cellular fibroblast-rich stroma raises HTRA1 and NOTCH3.
- Oedema, fluid-filled spaces, empty lumen, tissue edge: low total; TRBC1, IFI6, SLC15A4,
  LRRC41, LY6E, TP53INP1, CIB1 drop most.
- Haemorrhage (sheets of red cells) with some tumour around: total roughly unchanged or
  slightly lower, but HMOX1 (haem breakdown, macrophages), EGFL7 (endothelium), HTRA1,
  SCARB1, S100A9, CD14 (monocytes/neutrophils in blood), CD109, YWHAE are relatively higher;
  IFI27, LY6E, LRRC41, CIB1, HNRNPK lower. Pure blood clot with no cells: low everything.
  Haemosiderin-laden macrophages (golden-brown/black granules): CD14, GPNMB, HMOX1 up.
- Lymphocyte-rich patches (many small round dark nuclei, 6-8 um, little cytoplasm): TRBC1
  clearly up (+0.4, often 1.5-3 where lymphocytes dominate), TP53INP1 up (+0.4), CIB1,
  H3F3A, GADD45B, DNAJB12, SCARB1, TFRC slightly up; tumour genes (SLC39A14, NOL3, IFI27,
  HTRA1, PTGS1) down. Interferon genes (IFI6, ISG15, IRF7, PARP10) follow T-cell/immune
  infiltration at the tumour level.
- Neutrophils, foamy macrophages, necrotic debris: S100A9 and CD14 up, GPNMB, HMOX1, NAMPT
  up (myeloid/inflammatory group).
- Normal-looking tubules or atrophic tubules in fibrous tissue: low (like type B); slightly
  more CPD, FLNB, CKB, GADD45B, RXRA than surrounding collagen.

GENE GROUPS THAT MOVE TOGETHER (biology)
- Overall cellularity/"depth": all genes; strongest for IFI27, LY6E, LRRC41, CPE, CIB1, FLNB,
  PXN, SEMA4B, DERL1, HTRA1, CD109, VMP1, SLC15A4, CHST15, NAMPT. Weakest (more random):
  BLVRB, S100A9, EGFL7, BTF3, HMOX1, TP53INP1, YWHAE, IRF7, H3F3A.
- Interferon response: IFI6 <-> ISG15 strongest pair, plus IFI27, IRF7, PARP10, LY6E.
  High in inflamed high-grade tumour; low in bland or fibrotic tissue.
- Myeloid/macrophage/neutrophil: CD14, S100A9, GPNMB, HMOX1 (+NAMPT). High in inflamed,
  necrotic or bleeding areas; GPNMB also marks lipid-laden macrophages.
- T cells: TRBC1 with TP53INP1, H3F3A, some IFI6. Follows lymphocyte density.
- High-grade tumour-cell state: CPE, CKB, CD109, SEMA4B (+FLNB, CIB1, KRTCAP2).
- Well-differentiated clear-cell/vascular tumour: SLC39A14, LRRC41, TMEM91, NOL3, SCARB1,
  PTGS1, SLC15A4 (SLC39A14 <-> PTGS1 and SLC39A14 <-> TMEM91 pairs).
- Vessel/pericyte/stroma: NOTCH3, EGFL7, HTRA1 (HTRA1 highest in fibroblast-rich stroma).
- Proliferation/iron uptake/lysosome: TFRC, NT5DC2, LAPTM4B, DNAJB12 (NT5DC2 <-> TFRC pair).
  High in dense high-grade tumour.
- Housekeeping/stress: HSPA1A (heat-shock; relatively high in bland clear-cell sections and
  near haemorrhage), GADD45B, HNRNPK, YWHAE, BTF3, VMP1, DERL1.

PRACTICAL PROCEDURE FOR ONE PATCH
1. Ignore hue. Identify the tumour type (A-F) from nuclear grade, cytoplasm and architecture;
   look at several patches from the same case if available, since all share one type.
2. Start from that type's values (genes not listed: use the overall mean scaled by the
   type's level: A ~0.6-0.7x, B ~0.45x, C ~1.1x, D ~1.5x, E ~0.9x, F ~1.3x).
3. Scale by patch content: dense tumour 1.1-1.25x; tumour+stroma ~0.8x; stroma/fluid/
   blood-only 0.3-0.7x; nearly empty <0.3x.
4. Apply specific bumps: lymphocytes -> TRBC1, TP53INP1; blood/pigment -> HMOX1, CD14,
   EGFL7; neutrophils/debris -> S100A9, CD14; cellular fibroblastic stroma -> HTRA1, NOTCH3.
5. Typical levels: per-gene section means ran from ~0.1 to ~3.3; single spots above 3
   occurred only for CPE, HTRA1, SLC39A14, S100A9, CD14, CD109, IFI27, LY6E, IFI6.

TYPICAL SPOT TOTALS (sum of the 50 log values) per type, as a feel for overall level:
