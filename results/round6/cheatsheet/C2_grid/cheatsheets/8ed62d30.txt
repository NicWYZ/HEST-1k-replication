Train set: 814 spots. Composition: ~56% A tumor sheet, ~8% B tumor+stroma/immune,
~8% C basal epidermis/junction, ~5% D differentiated squamous epidermis, ~23% E low/acellular.
Decide the class per patch, then output that class's mean (table below). Blend

CLASSES (how they look at 0.5um/px, 224px = 112um):
A TUMOR SHEET: dense sheets/nests of large melanoma cells, round-oval vesicular
  nuclei, visible nucleoli, pale-pink/purple cytoplasm, little stroma. Also thin
  fibrovascular septa, vessels w/ RBCs, blood pools between nests. Highest VIM,
  POSTN, MGP, TYRP1, HAPLN1. If >30% white background, lower these by ~0.5-1.
B TUMOR + STROMA/IMMUNE: tumor nests separated by fibrous septa with many small
  dark round lymphocytes, vessels. Raises LYZ (5.8!), CD52, GZMK, LCK, ITM2A,
  WDFY4, BIRC3, IDO1, CCL19, VWF, MMRN1. More lymphocytes -> push further to B.
C BASAL EPIDERMIS / JUNCTION: keratinocyte layer (basal/spinous) abutting or mixed
  with melanoma nests, often clefts/clear cells. High KRT5/16/17, DSP, COL17A1,
  S100A14, TP63, MIR205HG, AQP3 but keratinocyte-differentiation genes lower than D.
D DIFFERENTIATED SQUAMOUS EPIDERMIS: stratified squamous epithelium, spinous/
  granular cells with abundant pink cytoplasm, intercellular bridges, flattened
  upper cells. Highest KRTDAP, DSC1, SBSN, DMKN, KRT2, DSP, SLPI, ACER1, DEFB1.
  Lowest melanoma/stroma genes (HAPLN1 ~0.6).
E LOW/ACELLULAR: mostly white background, cornified keratin flakes, hyaline
  homogeneous pink material (keratin/fibrin), loose fibrous strands, or sparse
  peripheral spindle stroma WITHOUT melanoma cells. Nearly everything ~0-1.3,
  regardless of how much tissue is in the patch. Small keratin pieces -> KRT16/17 ~2.

KEY RULES
- Tissue fraction matters: patches mostly white => class E values.
- Big-nucleus melanoma cells anywhere => VIM~6, POSTN~6, MGP~5-6, TYRP1~5.
- Any keratinocyte epithelium => KRT16/17/5, DSP high (6-7); melanoma genes drop
  to ~4 (VIM 4.3-4.9, TYRP1 ~4, POSTN 3-4, MGP 2-3).
- Immune cell density is the main driver of LYZ/CD52/GZMK/LCK/WDFY4/ITM2A.
- Overall gene means (fallback): VIM 4.9 POSTN 4.8 MGP 4.3 TYRP1 3.9 LYZ 2.7
  DMKN 2.5 HAPLN1 2.4 KRT16 2.4 KRT17 2.3 CRABP2 2.1 DSP 2.1 KRT5 2.0.
- Predict means not zeros: for uncertain genes use the class mean; low genes
  (~0.5) are fine at class mean.
