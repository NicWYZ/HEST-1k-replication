STEP 1 classify
A TUMOR: dense sheets/nests of large epithelioid cells, pale-pink cytoplasm, round vesicular
  nuclei w/ nucleoli, little white. Melanocyte/stroma genes high (VIM,POSTN,MGP,TYRP1,HAPLN1),
  keratinocyte genes low.
B TUMOR+IMMUNE/STROMA: tumor cells mixed w/ fibrous septa, vessels, small dark round lymphocytes,
  loose/myxoid stroma. Like A plus LYZ,CD52,WDFY4,ITM2A,LCK,GZMK,BIRC3,CCL19,VWF,MMRN1 higher.
C BASAL-EPI: viable squamous epithelium (basal/spinous layers, palisaded basal cells, often
  next to dermis/tumor). KRT5,TP63,COL17A1,MIR205HG,LAMB3 high; also some tumor/stroma signal.
D FULL-EPI: thick epidermis incl. granular/upper spinous, intercellular bridges, eosinophilic
  keratinocytes. All keratinocyte genes very high (DSP,KRT16/17/5,AQP3,S100A14 6-7).
E CORNIFIED/UPPER: pink lamellar keratin/stratum corneum with flattened cells, often with white
  gaps; SBSN,KRTDAP,SLPI,DMKN,KRT16 high but VIM/POSTN/TYRP1/MGP low.
F BLANK: mostly white background (>50% white) or acellular keratin/fibrin flake w/o nuclei.
  Everything low (~0-1.5).

- Scale by tissue coverage: each +10% white area in A/B lowers VIM/POSTN/MGP/TYRP1/LYZ by ~0.15-0.4.
  Patch 100% tissue, dense tumor: VIM~6.8,POSTN~6.8,MGP~6.5,TYRP1~5.8. Half white: ~4-5.
- Pigment (brown granules) -> TYRP1 up (to 6.5-7).
- Lymphocyte aggregate -> CD52,WDFY4,ITM2A 3-4, LCK,GZMK 2-3, CCL19 1.5+.
- Vessels/RBCs -> VWF 3-4, MMRN1 2-3 (lymphatic), SELE 1+, MYH11 1+ (smooth muscle).
- Epithelium mixed with tumor: blend rows proportional to area (e.g. 50% epi = avg of A and D).
- Keratin-only pink flake with few nuclei: use E or F, not D.
- Never predict 0 for everything in tissue; floor ~0.5 for low genes.
Gene correlations: keratinocyte block (DSP,KRT5/16/17,S100A14,MAL2,SERPINB5,AQP3,TP63,
COL17A1,MIR205HG,FGFBP1,RHOV,LY6D,CAPNS2,AHNAK2) moves together; upper-layer block
(SBSN,KRTDAP,SLPI,KRT2,DSC1,DEFB1,SERPINB2) peaks in granular/cornified; mesenchymal/tumor block
