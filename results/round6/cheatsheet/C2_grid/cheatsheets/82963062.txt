CHEATSHEET: predicting 50-gene expression (log1p counts, Xenium aggregated to 224x224 px spots, 0.5 um/px = 112 um patch) from H&E patches of human skin MELANOMA.
Built from one training section, 814 spots. Values below are log(1+count).

=== 1. WHAT THE TISSUE LOOKS LIKE / BIG PICTURE ===
The section is mostly a large nodular/nested MELANOMA (sheets and nests of big epithelioid cells: round-oval vesicular nuclei,
prominent nucleoli, abundant pale-pink/amphophilic cytoplasm, separated by thin pink fibrovascular septa). At the section edges there
is overlying/adjacent EPIDERMIS (stratified squamous: basal layer of small dark crowded nuclei, spinous layer of polygonal pink cells
with intercellular bridges, granular/cornified layer = flat bright-pink anucleate keratin sheets), plus lots of acellular bright-pink
keratin/cornified flakes, dense collagen and empty white background along the edges.
Proportions in training: ~56% melanoma nest core, ~8% tumor with immune-rich stroma, ~5% basal epidermis/junction, ~4% full epidermis,
~4% upper epidermis/cornified edge, ~22% near-empty / acellular (background, keratin flakes, dense pink collagen with few nuclei).

Expression is dominated by THREE gene programs (PCA: PC1 40%, PC2 23% of variance):
 (A) MELANOMA/STROMA program: VIM, POSTN, MGP, TYRP1, HAPLN1 (+ moderate LAMB3, BIRC3, IDO1, MYH11, HBD, TFF3, SELE). These are
     HIGH (5-7.5) wherever there is cellular tumor. TYRP1 = melanocytic marker; VIM/POSTN/MGP/HAPLN1 come from the tumor cells and
     the stroma. r(VIM,POSTN)=0.9, r(MGP,POSTN)=0.9, r(TYRP1,VIM)=0.9.
 (B) KERATINOCYTE/EPIDERMIS program: KRT16, KRT17, KRT5, DSP, S100A14, SERPINB5, MAL2, KRTDAP, SBSN, SLPI, COL17A1, TP63, AQP3,
     MIR205HG, LY6D, FGFBP1, DMKN, DSC1, DEFB1, RHOV, CAPNS2, AHNAK2, CRABP2, SERPINB2, ACER1, KRT2. All mutually correlated 0.7-0.9.
     Near 1-2 in tumor, 5-8 in epidermis. Sub-structure: BASAL genes (KRT5, COL17A1, TP63, MIR205HG, AQP3, LY6D, FGFBP1, LAMB3)
     peak in basal layer; DIFFERENTIATION genes (KRTDAP, SBSN, SLPI, DSC1, KRT2, DMKN, DEFB1, SERPINB2, ACER1) peak in suprabasal /
     granular layers. KRT16/KRT17/DSP/S100A14/SERPINB5/MAL2 are high throughout epidermis.
 (C) IMMUNE program: LYZ (myeloid, the strongest: up to 7), CD52, WDFY4, ITM2A, LCK, GZMK, CCL19, CCL22, CD1B, IDO1, BIRC3, IL1B
     plus endothelial VWF, MMRN1, SELE. Moderately co-vary with program A (immune cells sit in the tumor stroma). Highest where
     tumor nests are separated by fibrous septa containing small dark round lymphocyte nuclei, histiocytes, vessels.
Note: even blank/acellular patches have nonzero values (transcript spillover/diffusion): e.g. VIM~1.2, POSTN~1.1, KRT16~1-2.

=== 2. TISSUE CLASSES, VISUAL CUES, AND MEAN EXPRESSION ===
TUM   (56%) Melanoma nest core: dense sheets of large epithelioid cells, big vesicular nuclei with nucleoli, pinkish-lilac cytoplasm,
            little stroma, little white. Overall purple/lilac color (blue ~= red channel). Occasionally clear/myxoid areas.
TIMM  (8%)  Tumor + immune-rich stroma: tumor nests separated by pink fibrous septa/vessels with many SMALL DARK ROUND nuclei
            (lymphocytes), spindle fibroblasts, histiocytes. -> raise LYZ, CD52, WDFY4, ITM2A, GZMK, LCK, CCL19, VWF, MMRN1, BIRC3, IDO1.
BASEPI(5%)  Basal epidermis / junction with tumor: crowded basaloid keratinocytes, palisading basal layer, often next to melanoma
            cells or dermis; also hair follicle/adnexal epithelium. BOTH keratinocyte basal genes AND melanoma genes high.
EPI   (4%)  Full-thickness epidermis (spinous layer, polygonal pink cells with clear nuclear spacing, intercellular bridges, may
            include granular layer). Highest keratinocyte genes of all; tumor genes moderate (~2-5).
UPEPI (4%)  Upper epidermis/cornified edge: thin compressed squamous layer with flat elongated nuclei, bright-pink orthokeratin
            strands, white background on part of patch. Differentiation genes high (SLPI, SBSN, KRTDAP, KRT16/17), tumor genes low.
EMPTY (22%) Little/no cells: white background, keratin flakes, acellular bright pink keratin/collagen, few flat nuclei, blood
            debris. Everything low; keratin genes ~1-2 (spillover from nearby epidermis), tumor genes ~1.

=== 3. QUANTITATIVE FEATURE MODEL (if you can run Python/numpy) ===
Simple per-patch colour/nuclei features explain much of the variance. 5-fold CV mean Pearson r across the 50 genes:
linear ridge on the 12 features below = 0.55; random forest on same features = 0.66. Per-gene CV r (RF): VIM .93, POSTN .93,
MGP .90, TYRP1 .89, HAPLN1 .83, LYZ .80, BIRC3 .74, CRABP2 .72, IDO1 .72, LAMB3 .71, KRTDAP .70, keratin genes ~.6-.7,
immune minor genes (CCL22, CD1B, HBD, SELE, TFF3, MMRN1, IL1B) only ~.4-.5 (predict close to mean for those).
Feature code (patch = RGB uint8 224x224):
  import numpy as np; from scipy import ndimage as ndi; from skimage.color import rgb2hed
  a=img.astype(float); r,g,b=a[...,0],a[...,1],a[...,2]; mx=a.max(-1); mn=a.min(-1); gray=a.mean(-1)
  white=(mn>200)&(mx-mn<25); tis=~white                      # background mask
  hed=rgb2hed(a/255.); H=hed[...,0][tis].mean(); E=hed[...,1][tis].mean()   (0 if no tissue)
  bvsr=(b-r)[tis].mean()          # blue minus red over tissue: + = purple/cellular tumor; very negative = bright pink keratin/collagen
  sat=(mx-mn)[tis].mean()
  nuc=(gray<140)&(b>r-10); lab,n=ndi.label(ndi.binary_opening(nuc)); sizes=ndi.sum(nuc,lab,range(1,n+1))
  nucfrac=nuc.mean(); nnuc=(sizes>30).sum()
  darkfrac=(gray<100).mean(); gray_sd=gray.std(); white=white.mean()
  vd=gray<110; l,n=ndi.label(vd); s=ndi.sum(vd,l,range(1,n+1)); darksmall=((s>=15)&(s<=120)).sum()   # lymphocyte-like blobs
  nuc2=(gray<150)&(b>r-15); l2,n2=ndi.label(ndi.binary_opening(nuc2)); s2=ndi.sum(nuc2,l2,range(1,n2+1)); bignuc=(s2>150).sum()
  pinkfib=((r>200)&(g<150)&(b>170)&(b<215)&(r-b>15)).mean()  # pink collagen/keratin pixels

=== 4. RECOMMENDED PREDICTION RECIPE (vision or code) ===
Step 1. Estimate, for each patch, the area fractions: f_tum (melanoma cells, incl. septa), f_imm (immune-rich stroma/lymphoid
        aggregates), f_basal (basal/basaloid keratinocytes), f_epi (spinous/granular epidermis), f_corn (cornified keratin layer),
        f_empty (white background / acellular). They sum to 1.
Step 2. Prediction ~= weighted mix of class profiles in the table: TUM*f_tum + TIMM*f_imm + BASEPI*f_basal + EPI*f_epi +
        UPEPI*f_corn + EMPTY*f_empty (mixing in log space is fine as an approximation). Patches with ANY epidermis get a big
        keratin boost: even ~30% epidermis area gives KRT16/KRT17/DSP ~5-6.
Step 3. Refinements:
  - Pure, densely cellular melanoma with no white: VIM ~6.3-6.8, POSTN ~6.3-6.8, MGP ~6, TYRP1 ~5-5.5, HAPLN1 ~4-4.5,
    LYZ ~3, keratin genes ~1-1.7 (KRT16 ~1.7, DSP ~1.5, KRT5 ~1.3), immune minor genes ~0.5-1.
  - Tumor with partial white gaps/tears (up to ~50% white) but the remaining tissue clearly purple melanoma: tumor genes stay
    high (VIM~6, POSTN~6, MGP~5.5, TYRP1~4.7; HAPLN1 drops to ~2-3). Only when >50% white do they fall to ~3-4. White patches
    at the pink/keratin edge (no tumor) -> VIM/POSTN ~1-2 (see lookup in section 7).
  - Visible lymphocyte clusters / perivascular cuffs / fibrous septa in tumor: LYZ 5-6.5, CD52 2.5-3.5, WDFY4 2.5-3, ITM2A 2.5-3,
    GZMK 2-2.5, LCK 2-2.5, BIRC3 3-3.5, IDO1 2-2.5, CCL19 1.5-2, VWF 2.5-3, MMRN1 ~2 (vessels/lymphatics), CD1B ~1, SELE ~1.
  - HAPLN1 is most tumor-cell specific (high in pure nests, ~3.7 TUM vs 2.2 TIMM, ~0.5 epidermis).
  - Epidermis basal layer present: KRT5 ~6-6.5, COL17A1 ~5.5, TP63 ~4-5, MIR205HG ~4-4.5, AQP3 ~4.5-6, LY6D 2-3, LAMB3 ~4.
  - Spinous/granular layer present: KRTDAP 4.5-6, SBSN 5.5-6.5, SLPI 5-5.5, DSC1 3-6, KRT2 up to 4 (KRT2 is specific to upper
    spinous/granular layer, otherwise ~1), DMKN 5-6, DEFB1 2-3, SERPINB2 ~3, ACER1 ~3.
  - Pure cornified flakes / acellular keratin with no nucleated epidermis: low everything (KRT16 ~2, SLPI ~1, SBSN ~1.2).
  - Totally blank white patch: all genes ~0-1 (VIM~1.2, POSTN~1.1, MGP~0.9, TYRP1~1, KRT16~1, DSP~0.9, others ~0.1-0.5).
  - Dense pink collagen with few spindle nuclei (dermis): VIM ~2-4, POSTN ~2-4, MGP ~2-3, low keratin and immune.
  - Melanin pigment was rare in this section; TYRP1 is high in all melanoma cells regardless of visible pigment.
Step 4. When uncertain, shrink toward the overall mean (ALL column): predicting the mean is far better than a confident wrong
        tissue call. Correlated genes should move together (use program structure above).

=== 5. GENE NOTES ===
VIM: highest overall gene in tumor (median 6.1); POSTN (median 5.9), MGP (5.4), TYRP1 (4.7) - bimodal: ~6 in tumor, ~1-2 off tumor.
LYZ: median 2.7, 5.7 in immune-rich tumor stroma, ~4 at basal epidermis/junction (dermal inflammation), ~1 in upper epidermis.
CRABP2, DMKN, LAMB3, AHNAK2, CAPNS2: expressed in BOTH tumor (~1.7-2.6) and epidermis (~4-6) -> intermediate everywhere.
BIRC3/IDO1/GZMK/LCK/CCL19/CCL22/CD1B/WDFY4/ITM2A/CD52: immune; ~1 in tumor core, 2-3 in immune stroma, ~0 in empty.
MYH11 (smooth muscle/pericytes), HBD, TFF3, SELE, IL1B: low (0.5-1) almost everywhere; slightly higher in tumor/stroma (~0.8-1).
KRT16 and KRT17 are almost interchangeable (r=0.9); likewise SBSN~KRTDAP~SLPI, KRT5~COL17A1~TP63~AQP3~MIR205HG.
Gene max values seen: KRT16 8.4, SBSN 8.2, KRT17 8.1, KRT5 8.0, DSP 7.9, MGP 7.9, DMKN 7.8, S100A14 7.6, VIM 7.5, POSTN 7.4.
