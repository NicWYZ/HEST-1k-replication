CHEATSHEET: predicting 50-gene log1p expression from H&E patches (224px, 0.5um/px) of one melanoma section.
Values are log(1+count) per spot; ranges 0..~8. Predict conservatively (near class mean) when unsure: a wrong extreme costs more than a mean.

BIG PICTURE (two axes explain ~76% of variance across spots)
1) Tumor/stromal axis (PC ~29%): melanoma cells + desmoplastic stroma + immune infiltrate. HIGH: POSTN, VIM, MGP, TYRP1 (melanocytic), HAPLN1, LYZ, LAMB3, BIRC3, VWF, plus immune genes (CD52, LCK, GZMK, IDO1, WDFY4, ITM2A, CCL19, CCL22, CD1B, HBD, SELE, TFF3, MYH11, MMRN1, IL1B - all low-level, 0.5-1.5).
2) Keratinocyte axis (PC ~47%): squamous epidermis / keratinising surface. HIGH: KRT16, KRT17, KRT5, DSP, SBSN, S100A14, SLPI, KRTDAP, AQP3, SERPINB5, MAL2, DSC1, COL17A1, TP63, SERPINB2, LY6D, KRT2, DMKN, CRABP2, MIR205HG, ACER1, AHNAK2, CAPNS2, FGFBP1, RHOV, DEFB1.
The two axes are roughly anti-correlated: tumor patches have high POSTN/VIM/MGP/TYRP1/HAPLN1 and low keratin genes; epidermis patches the reverse. Blank patches are low on everything.

WHAT THE SLIDE LOOKS LIKE
- Most of the section (~2/3 of patches) is dense melanoma: nests of large round cells with vesicular nuclei and nucleoli, pale pink/purple cytoplasm, separated by thin pink fibrous septa with spindle fibroblasts and lymphocytes. Strongly purple/dense nuclei = high tumor+stroma genes.
- Edges of the section contain epidermis with thick pink keratin (hyperkeratosis, parakeratosis, stratified squamous cells with small round nuclei in a glassy bright-pink matrix, few nuclei, smooth texture). These carry the keratin genes.
- Glass/white background and fat/lumen/tissue-fold edges appear as pale grey-white; patches that are >50% white get near-zero for almost all genes (but 0.5-1.5 for KRT16/KRT17/DSP/POSTN/VIM/MGP, ambient signal).

HOW TO CLASSIFY A PATCH (visual rules; use fractions, patches are mixtures)
- empty: >90% white/grey background -> use column empty.
- blank: 50-90% white, little tissue -> column blank.
- keratin: bright, smooth pink (high eosin, low edge/texture, few and sparse nuclei, low nuclear hematoxylin), stratified squamous or cornified layers -> column keratin. Fully keratinised pink strands with no nuclei still have KRT16/KRT17/KRT5/DSP/SBSN high (3-4.5) and POSTN/VIM low (~2).
- sparse: partially pale tissue, 25-50% white, thin/folded tissue -> column sparse.
- tumor: dense cellular tissue, many nuclei, textured -> column tumor.
For mixed patches interpolate linearly between columns by the area fraction of each tissue type.

WITHIN-TUMOR MODULATION
- More nuclei / texture (dark purple, dense stroma+lymphocytes, strong edges) -> raise immune genes (GZMK, IDO1, LCK, CD52, WDFY4, CCL19, CCL22, BIRC3, LYZ ~+0.3..+0.8) and HAPLN1/POSTN/MGP/VIM.
- Tumor patch that is smoother, pinker, with few nuclei (transition to epidermis/keratin) -> lower POSTN/MGP/VIM/HAPLN1/TYRP1 (toward 2-3 for POSTN, ~1 for HAPLN1), raise KRT16/KRT17/KRT5/DSP/SBSN/SLPI/KRTDAP/S100A14/MAL2 (toward 3-4).
- HAPLN1 is the most tumor-specific (keratin/blank ~0.2-0.4, tumor 3-4; patchy). TYRP1 follows melanocyte tumor cells (4.5-5.5 in tumor, ~2 in epidermis). MGP/POSTN/VIM: 5.5-6.5 in tumor regardless of subtype; fairly uniform, so predict ~6 for any dense tumor patch.
- Simple image features (RGB, saturation, white fraction, hematoxylin, edge density) with ridge regression gave cross-validated r ~0.9 for VIM/POSTN/MGP/TYRP1, ~0.8 for HAPLN1, ~0.65-0.75 for BIRC3/LYZ/IDO1/GZMK/LCK, ~0.45-0.55 for most keratin genes. Keratin genes and sparse immune genes are the hardest; shrink toward class means.
- Sparse genes (CD1B, CCL19, CCL22, SELE, TFF3, MYH11, HBD, IL1B, DEFB1, LY6D, ACER1) have means 0.4-1; predict small values (0.3-1.0) everywhere except slightly higher in lymphocyte-rich/stromal tumor patches. Zeros are common.
- Gene groups move together; if you decide a patch is keratinocyte-like, set the whole keratin group consistently (KRT16~KRT17~KRT5~DSP~SBSN~S100A14~SLPI; KRTDAP, SERPINB5, MAL2, DSC1, COL17A1, AQP3 follow at lower levels), and if tumor-like set POSTN~VIM~MGP~TYRP1 high together.
