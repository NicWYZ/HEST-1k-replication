B [22% of spots] Sparse, pale or blank

C [8% of spots] Dense immune-rich infiltrate
   Many small dark round nuclei packed together, lymphocyte/macrophage-like, often mixed with tumour cells. Immune and dendritic-cell genes are highest here.
D [4% of spots] Cornified squamous epithelium
   Thick, dense eosinophilic (bright pink) keratin layers with flattened or pyknotic nuclei. Epidermal differentiation genes high.
E [5% of spots] Tumour nests with basal/squamous features and fibrous stroma

F [4% of spots] Non-cornified squamous epithelium
   Stratified epithelium of spinous keratinocytes: round nuclei, pale cytoplasm, layered, no thick keratin. Moderate keratinocyte genes, low tumour genes (TYRP1, VIM, POSTN).

4. VISUAL CUES THAT SHIFT VALUES WITHIN AN ARCHETYPE
- Amount of tissue: the more of the patch is blank white, the lower almost every gene. Keep it near archetype B.
- Keratin content (dense pink cornified or layered epithelium): raises KRT*, SBSN, AQP3, DSP, DMKN, S100A14, KRTDAP.
- Stromal fibrous pink with elongated nuclei: raises POSTN, MGP, HAPLN1, MYH11, VIM.
- Dense small dark nuclei and lymphocytes: raises LYZ, CD52, LCK, GZMK, CCL19, IDO1, WDFY4.
- Pale, large-nucleus tumour nests (no keratin): TYRP1, VIM high; keratin genes low.
- Overall darker, more stained patches correlate with higher keratinocyte genes and lower LYZ/POSTN.
