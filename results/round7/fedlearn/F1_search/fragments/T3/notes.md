## Q1. Is accuracy limited by data?
- **No learning curve in training patients or sites for spatial prediction was found** within these sources and queries (q01, q10, q11 on all sources: PubMed 4, 5, 1 hits; arXiv 0 hits; Europe PMC top 100 screened by title). HEST-1k (arXiv:2406.16192), STPath (10.1038/s41746-025-02020-3), STFlow (arXiv:2506.05361), HESCAPE (arXiv:2508.01490), Wang 2025 (10.1038/s41467-025-56618-y) report none; STPath and STFlow were keyword-searched in full text, not read cover to cover.
- **Bulk prediction does show data-size effects.** SEQUOIA (10.1038/s41467-024-54182-5): well-predicted genes track training size across 16 cancers, and downsampling BRCA to 20/30/40% lowered all metrics (confounded by cancer type across cancers). HE2RNA (10.1038/s41467-020-17678-4) varied the split between a representation-learning hospital and a target hospital (388 to 78 patients) for MSI transfer.
- **Encoder-side scaling is weak for expression.** HEST-1k: log model size R=0.81, pretraining patches R=0.48 (P=0.13). arXiv:2410.00945: seven top encoders spanning ~21K to 3.1M pretraining slides sit within 0.571-0.596 mean PAM50 Pearson. Nat Commun 2026 benchmark (10.1038/s41467-026-76004-6): scaling clear on TCGA multi-class, weak on external sets (classification tasks). ELF (arXiv:2508.16085) and Virchow (10.1038/s41591-024-03141-0) show diminishing returns in H&E pretraining size; TITAN ran a 12.5-100% sweep (values not extracted).
- **Representation, platform, biology.** arXiv:2609.32857: gene means carry much of aggregate score, within-slide variation is the unresolved part, and a closed-form affine head matches the best models on HEST-1k cohorts. Skin benchmark (10.64898/2026.09.07.749926, abstract only): ridge on embeddings equals complex models, near-zero accuracy on most genes. Data quality paper (PMID 40964396): Xenium beats Visium; sparsity and noise degrade prediction. HESCAPE: batch effects, and contrastive pretraining lowered direct expression prediction.
- **Cross-cohort transfer.** Wang 2025: weak transfer across subtypes and resolutions. arXiv:2410.00945: TCGA-BRCA to SCAN-B (997 patients) mean PAM50 Pearson 0.443. arXiv:2606.29949: R@10 fell to 10.8% and 4.0% on unseen TCGA-LUAD/BRCA; 8 target pairs (5%) lifted LUAD to 43.5%. Path2Omics (abstract only): frozen-section model beat FFPE model on FFPE external sets. Not found: any study training on several named centers and testing on held-out centers for ST (q05, q09, q22 on all sources).

## Q2. What data do centers hold?
- Paired H&E + spatial: HEST-1k 1,229 samples / 153 cohorts / 2.1M pairs; STimage-1K4M 1,149 slides / 4.29M pairs; OmiCLIP 2.2M pairs; HumanST-1k 1.8M pairs (arXiv:2606.03644); STORM 632 sections; SpaRED 26 Visium sets; STHELAR 31 Xenium sections. These pools overlap and differ by panel and platform; I did not de-duplicate them.
- Paired H&E + bulk RNA: TCGA via HE2RNA (8,725 patients, 28 cancers), SEQUOIA (7,584 tumours plus CPTAC, Tempus 287 slides), arXiv:2410.00945 (four TCGA cohorts, SCAN-B 997), arXiv:2606.29949 (1,720). GTEx appears only in GHIST+ (abstract only).
- H&E alone: Virchow ~1.5M slides, Prov-GigaPath 171,189 slides / 28 centres / >30,000 patients, Campanella 423k slides, UNI >100k slides.
- Ways to enter a model: supervised head on ST pairs (HEST-bench), bulk weak supervision (HE2RNA, tRNAsformer, STGAT, BiSCALE 2,109 bulk + 141k spots, RNA-guided contrastive training), spot-level supervision to finer scale (sCellST), reference retrieval (BLEEP, Loki), self-supervised H&E pretraining. Not found: federated or multi-center treatment of any of these (q01-q09 plus q21 "multi-site federated learning pathology gene expression").

## Discrepancies and limits
- HEST-1k counts differ by version: 1,108 samples / 131 cohorts (v1), 1,229 / 153 (v2, NeurIPS), 1,276 on the GitHub page. Seed description matches v2.
- The scan says none of the accuracy-and-scale seeds reports a learning curve. That holds for the ST papers; SEQUOIA (bulk) does report a downsampling result, and HE2RNA a sample-split experiment.
- arXiv queries are strict AND of terms; q01, q05, q08, q09 returned 0, so four looser arXiv rows (q01b, q05b, q08b, q09b) were added. arXiv returned 429 repeatedly; all rows eventually succeeded.
- PubMed q02 had to be re-run with "H E" for "H&E". OpenReview API (api2.openreview.net) was not reachable; PMLR returned no proceedings page; both are covered only by web-search wording (q24, q25).
- Europe PMC rows screened first 100 by relevance at title level, then abstracts for shortlisted papers; many hits are 2026 items of marginal relevance. Included counts per row are title matches, so they are provisional.
- Not covered: Semantic Scholar; Kather, Fu and other mutation-prediction pan-cancer papers (not expression); iStar and TESLA-type super-resolution papers; sizes of private institutional archives. Read levels: "full text" means retrieved and read at the passages needed, not cover to cover.
