## Q8. Alternatives to federated learning
**Retrieval in a joint embedding space.** BLEEP (NeurIPS 2023; arXiv:2306.01859, full text) is the primary reference: one human liver Visium dataset, no privacy or multi-site analysis. Relatives found: EGN (arXiv:2210.16721), mclSTExp (10.1093/bib/bbae551), OmiCLIP (10.1038/s41592-025-02707-1), DKAN (arXiv:2511.17685), HECLIP, GR2ST, ST-ConMa. Two papers carry usable evidence. HESCAPE (arXiv:2508.01490, full text): contrastive pretraining improved mutation classification but lowered direct expression prediction relative to baseline encoders, with batch effects named as the cause; folds are patient-level. VOICE (arXiv:2608.08366, full text, not in the oversight scan): a direct branch plus retrieval from reference cells; fusion beat the direct branch in all 21 slide-level comparisons in the 24-slide model (macro PCC 0.3810 to 0.3904). No paper was found that stores a retrieval database across institutions or measures its privacy cost (queries T7-q01, q02, q15 and the cross-check q17, which returned nothing on federated spatial transcriptomics).

**Textual and agent-written knowledge bases.** Nothing found applies a text knowledge base or cheatsheet to histology-to-expression prediction (T7-q03, q04, q10 on PubMed, Europe PMC, arXiv, web, NeurIPS and PMLR titles returned unrelated hits). The nearest published relatives are from other domains: Dynamic Cheatsheet (arXiv:2504.07952, full text; gains reported in reasoning tasks), ACE (arXiv:2510.04618), ExpeL (arXiv:2308.10144), in-context regression (arXiv:2404.07544; arXiv:2405.12856), TabLLM (arXiv:2210.10723), GenePT (10.1038/s41551-024-01284-6), and the RAG reference (arXiv:2005.11401). All are abstract level except Dynamic Cheatsheet. Round 6's own results (docs/round06/README.md, "What the round established") were read and are the project's evidence for this route; none of the papers above contradicts or replicates it.

**Central scaling of public data.** STPath (10.1038/s41746-025-02020-3, abstract only), OmiCLIP, SToFM (PMLR v267), STFlow (ICML 2025; arXiv:2506.05361, abstract) pool public data. The only data-scale comparison found in the sources searched is VOICE: 24 against 75 Xenium slides, improvement 0.013 to 0.047 per metric, per-gene Pearson across all cells of a slide (offsets not removed). HEST-1k (arXiv:2406.16192, full text) reports a model-size scaling trend over 11 encoders (R = 0.81) but the sections read give no learning curve in training patients or centers. This differs from the oversight scan, which found none; the VOICE result is a two-point curve in training slides, not centers.

**Better or fine-tuned encoders.** SEAL (arXiv:2602.14177, full text): all five encoders improved on HEST-Bench, by at most 1.33% average PCC. Robustifying fine-tuning (arXiv:2607.22861, full text): HEST mean Pearson 0.398 to 0.411, 9 of 10 models improved. PEKA (arXiv:2504.07061, abstract), MINT (arXiv:2603.07895), Meta-Encoder (10.1038/s41467-026-71558-x, abstract; its abstract says missing data sharing makes pooled retraining infeasible, which the lead may want under federation), Neidlinger benchmark (10.1038/s41551-025-01516-3). Encoder gains reported are a few percent on the HEST-Bench head.

## Q9. Public stand-ins and evaluation
**Sources of centers.** HEST-1k, STimage-1K4M (arXiv:2406.06393; 1,149 slides), SpaRED (26 Visium datasets), HESCAPE (6 panels, 54 donors, panels mix organs and sites), STHELAR (31 Xenium sections). None offers a laboratory contrast with disease fixed, as the project already found for HEST-1k. TCGA tissue source sites: Howard et al. (10.1038/s41467-021-24698-1, full text) show site signatures in more than 3,000 patients and propose preserved-site cross-validation, directly relevant to leave-one-center-out; Kömen et al. (arXiv:2411.05489, full text; PathoROB 10.1038/s41467-026-73923-2, abstract) show foundation-model features still carry site signatures; ComBat on deep features (PMID:39398947, abstract). No paper found that simulates federated centers from HEST-1k labs or studies (T7-q06, q08, q13 returned nothing specific).
**Metric.** STRank (NeurIPS 2025, proceedings page https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/7d535a224c8ae54ba75bac0457b6b279-Abstract-Conference.html, abstract only) argues for relative rather than absolute expression because of batch effects; it is the only metric-level paper found. Wang et al. (10.1038/s41467-025-56618-y, full text) reports low PCC across methods in HER2+ ST data. Whether any of these papers removes slide-level offsets was not checked.

## Discrepancies and caveats
- Oversight scan said no paper gave a learning curve; VOICE gives a 24-vs-75-slide comparison (single-cell Xenium, not centers). HEST-1k gives model-size scaling only.
- Wang et al. 2025 is cited in the scan as Nat Commun 16:1544; Crossref record confirms Nature Communications 2025, DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-56618-y; volume and article number were not checked.
- BLEEP's venue and HEST-1k's, STFlow's are confirmed from proceedings pages; EGN, ExpeL, TabLLM and RAG venues are not (marked unconfirmed in evidence.csv).
- GenePT: Crossref year 2024, Europe PMC year 2025.
- PPML-Omics and privateST (federated items) belong to other themes and were not searched here.

## Not covered or reduced
- OpenReview search was unreachable (API outside the allowlist); NeurIPS 2023-2025 and PMLR ICML 2023-2025, MIDL 2023-2024 were matched on titles only; other PMLR volumes were not searched.
- arXiv API was queried with AND of words (not phrases) and hit HTTP 429 repeatedly; all queries finally completed after retries. The first pass of PubMed and Europe PMC failed on parsing and was rerun; counts in the log are from the rerun.
- PubMed summaries were read for the first 40 hits only (only T7-q15 has more than 40, with 64 hits). Europe PMC titles were scanned to rank 100. Web search screened one results page per query; extension queries q09 to q17 were not run on the web.
- Web queries q02, q03, q07 and q08 used the listed string plus extra words (noted in the log).
- Most papers beyond the core set are abstract level; summaries of those rest on abstracts.
