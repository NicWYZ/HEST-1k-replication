## Q3. What would be federated
- Route 1, summary statistics in one round. No paper in T2 fits a ridge or linear head from per-site sums to histology-to-expression. Nearest: FedDBL (arXiv 2302.12662, full text): one-round closed-form head on frozen features for tile classification (4.6 GB to 276.5 KB per client). Method precedents excluded under the natural-image rule but worth the lead's attention for the sums-based head: FedOPAL (arXiv 2607.08368; closed form from summed client autocorrelation and cross-correlation matrices), Exact Federated Continual Unlearning for Ridge Heads on Frozen Foundation Models (arXiv 2603.12977; the ridge head depends on data only through additive sufficient statistics F^T F and F^T Y, verified in the text; benchmarks CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, FeMNIST, Sentiment140; retrain-equivalent within 1e-9), and Parametric Feature Transfer (arXiv 2402.01862; one-shot, frozen foundation model, Gaussian-mixture feature sharing; the paper itself shows sending real features can be reconstructed). Medical one-shot items seen: DP-CVAE on foundation-model embeddings (arXiv 2507.02671, full text), PMID 40674892 (one-shot knowledge distillation), FBPFT-VI (snippet only).
- Route 2, iterative nonlinear head on frozen embeddings. Cong (10.1038/s41746-026-02710-6, full text): about 1K parameters per round, communication 3.16 MB to 1.6 KB reported, AUC gains of 0.11-0.13 over local-only. Lu (full text): attention-MIL on frozen tile features, federated AUC 0.862 vs 0.919 (breast) and 0.976 vs 0.985 (kidney), three simulated TCGA sites. Saldanha (full text): real three-country swarm, QUASAR MSI AUROC 0.8326 swarm vs 0.8308 merged. TNBC response from H&E under FL (PMID 36658418; medRxiv text read): collaborative training improved over local WSI models (0.75 vs local on cohort A; 0.60 vs 0.57 on cohort B). Federated HER2 status from H&E (arXiv 2312.06454; four sites, 2,687 WSIs; text read). Histo-Cloud (bioRxiv 10.1101/2021.08.17.456679; kidney IFTA segmentation, three institutions, comparable to pooled).
- Route 3, PEFT of an encoder. Evidence is from radiology and general imaging: arXiv 2407.11573 (full text; limited efficacy on heterogeneous/out-of-domain data; about 4% accuracy lost per ten-fold parameter cut), arXiv 2609.02101 (full text; mean AUC 0.687 to 0.802 vs 0.812 pooled, naive factor averaging costs 0.097), arXiv 2608.16268 (CoM3eT; under 2.5% of parameters matched full fine-tuning, federated hospital training comparable to pooled), arXiv 2407.21739, 10.1038/s41746-026-02866-1. For pathology encoders: FedHisto-PAST (arXiv 2609.31150; frozen HIBOU-B, five-client simulation, abstract only). No federated fine-tuning of a pathology foundation model at real multi-site scale was found.
- Route 4, federated pretraining. UltraFedFM (10.1038/s41746-025-02085-0, full text; 16 clients, 9 countries, 1,015,754 unlabeled ultrasound images), BrainFedFM (arXiv 2609.27611; 42 sites, 164,707 MRI scans), 10.1109/TMI.2023.3323540 (synthetic images), arXiv 2505.24108, F2Med (Research Square preprint). None for pathology foundation models at scale.
- Federated expression prediction. Only PPML-Omics (10.1126/sciadv.adh8601; full text read is the bioRxiv preprint). Queries q16 and q18 across PubMed, Europe PMC, arXiv API and web search found nothing else. Not searched: differing gene panels across sites and federated reference databases (C4).
- Real federations: PMC12357140 (melanoma, three clients, four networks in four countries; the federated model was best across clients but not better than every local model on its own test set); TheODen (10.1038/s41746-026-02958-y; three German university hospitals; Dice 0.764 BCSS, 0.754 SemiCOL); FeTS (71 sites; 27%/33%/16% gain over the public initial model on local validation data); EXAM (20 institutes; AUC >0.92); CAIMEN (PMID 37625894); SPIE 2026 four-institution pilot (10.1117/12.3088236; near-parity Dice, abstract page only).

## Q5. Privacy and governance
- Identifying content: H&E slides can be matched to their patient (Ganz, 10.1016/j.media.2024.103335). **Version discrepancy**: the arXiv text (read in full) reports F1 of 50.16%, 52.30% and 62.31%; the published abstract reports up to 80.1%, 77.2% and 77.1%. State which version a number comes from. WSIs without metadata can still be linked through image matching (Kanwal, arXiv 2307.09426); scanner formats carry identifying metadata (Bisson, 10.1177/20552076231171475). Pixel-level identifiers in imaging: PMID 41295085 (abstract only). Nothing in T2 on re-identification from expression profiles or summary statistics.
- Attacks: gradient inversion impractical when batch-norm statistics are updated (Hatamizadeh, arXiv version read); MedLeak recovers images from aggregated updates (arXiv 2407.09972); SRATTA attacks secure aggregation and groups samples by client (arXiv 2306.07644; evaluated on CIFAR10, FashionMNIST, a DNA dataset and multi-centric tabular TCGA-BRCA, not slides); FedSynthCT security analysis (PMID 42400864): FedMIA AUC 0.92-0.99, secure aggregation reduced it to 0.23-0.56; Nature 2026 patient-level membership-inference audit (10.1038/s41586-026-10688-0): attacks on trained models, federated/swarm learning gives no protection against them. No attack on sums such as X^T X and X^T Y was found in T2.
- DP cost: Adnan (full text) pooled 0.839 / FL 0.823 / DP-FL 0.823 at epsilon 2.90, external 0.768 / 0.741 / 0.707; DP scoping review (PMID 41484344; 74 studies; acceptable near epsilon 10, large loss near 1); FedDP (arXiv 2411.04509; small Dice cost, abstract only); Ziller (full text; DP chest-radiograph model still underperformed at epsilon about 0.5-2.8; figures not re-extracted); Schoenpflug review cites DP about -6% vs SMC about -0.5% in one comparison.
- Practice and governance: FeTS, EXAM, TheODen, EUCAIM (PMID 42006397; data sharing and transfer agreements, data access committee, de facto anonymisation), PDA principles (PMID 42440280), MedPerf (10.1038/s42256-023-00652-2). MELLODDY: only press releases and a snippet were seen, Heyndrickx (JCIM 2023) was not opened and is not in the table. No data-use agreement document was found.
