# Round 8 positioning track, checks

`williams_vs_r5.py` checks section 3.2 of the positioning note by exact enumeration. For a toy population of 10 clusters with a nonlinear relation between the cluster contributions $z$ and the mean predictions $f$, it runs over every simple random sample of $n$ clusters and every equal split into two halves. It asserts that round 5's cross-fitted estimator equals $T_1 - T_2$ and that Williams' (1961) estimator with two groups equals $T_1 - (1 - n/G)\,T_2$, then reports each estimator's bias and standard deviation over the design. `williams_vs_r5_output.txt` is its output. Run with `python3 williams_vs_r5.py` (needs numpy).
