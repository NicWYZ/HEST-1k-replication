results/tailored - diagnostics we designed, not configurations from the paper.

Grouped by the question each answers; every file is produced by the same-named script under
code/scripts/.

  splits/          what a split design costs. Four designs x 5 repeats x 10 tasks, reported in
                   WITHIN-PATIENT Pearson because pooled Pearson scales with test-set
                   heterogeneity and is not a constant yardstick across designs.
  site_probes/     whether slide, institution, assay and cohort are recoverable from frozen
                   features (the exchangeability test). Includes the spatial-block variant that
                   removes the adjacent-tissue shortcut.
  shift/           distribution shift: leave-cohort-source-out, size-matched slide novelty, and
                   across-task, each a different contrast and reported separately.
  counts/          per-gene Poisson / NB / ZINB fits by AIC - which observation model the target
                   counts support.
  morphology/      per-spot CellViT nuclear features over the true patch box, plus the HEST
                   Figure 3.e reproduction gate and the patch-scale calibration sources.
  regularization/  ridge penalty sweep with Gram condition numbers.
  genes/           held-out-gene check, with the penalty held fixed so gene subsetting is
                   separated from the regularisation change the benchmark's formula induces.
  integrity/       verification of the instrumentation layer against independent references,
                   the sample-metadata join, and the Stage 5 integration check.
