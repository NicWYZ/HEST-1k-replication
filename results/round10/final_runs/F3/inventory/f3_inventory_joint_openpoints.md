**The cheapest-valid-set rule.** Two different rules exist.
1. Round 4's rule is a fixed function of o, not of data: `hcp` at o = 0, `ghcp` for 0 < o < 9, `within_plain` for 9 <= o < 25, `within` from o = 25. It is defined in `docs/round04/tracks/ppi/round4_ppi_plan.md` section 14.9 (heading at line 979; item 4 at lines 997 to 1002, "marked by the conformal track's rule") and coded in `round4_ppi_q5_joint.py` lines 45 to 46. It has no `within_full` entry. Where m is not on the o grid, the nearest o at or below m is used (`o_used`, line 62).
2. Round 5's rule is data-driven, `narrowest_valid`: per (task, part, alpha, K, o), the method with a finite-sample guarantee (hcp, one_per, ghcp, ghcp_noad, within, within_plain, within_full) that is finite in every row and has the smallest `width_mean`, excluding `one_per` (`round5_conf_w3_merge.py` docstring lines 41 to 48, code lines 227 to 231). `round5_ppi_e5_joint.py` uses it (line 60). The brief's wording (GHCP and `within_full` at o = m, every K) fits round 5's rule and round 5's W3 grids, not round 4's.

**(g) Gaps for item 4**
1. No earlier script computes the prediction-set side from LOO prediction files. C3/W3 refit heads from embeddings per (fold, calibration draw). Running them "on the final prediction files" needs either their unchanged refit path (same embeddings, which reproduces round 5 rather than using the final files) or a new design that conditions on the LODO predictions, whose calibration donors are not held out from the test donor's head.
2. `within_full` exists only in W3 (HEST tasks). It is not in round 4's ACS conformal code (`round4_conf_c3_acs_o.py`), and round 5's census W2 script (`round5_conf_w2_census.py`, with `within_full`) is the released PUMA experiment, not ACS by state. ACS by state has no `within_full` implementation.
3. Kidney Visium (corpus, G = 51) has no conformal loader in C3/W3 (TASKS covers four HEST variants only). Its regime B rows exist in F2.
4. K grids: W3 sweeps K on CCRCC, CCRCC_merged (10 to 20) and INDIANA_KIDNEY (10, 14, 18, 20) only; LUNG_XENIUM has K = 10 only in W3 (C3 had 6, 8, 10 without `within_full`); ACS K is fixed by fold size (7 to 10). "Every K the task allows" is not on file for lung, ACS or kidney Visium.
5. o and m grids do not coincide: F2 regime B m (3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 20, 30); W3 part 1 o (3, 5, 9, 10, 12, 15, 17, 20, 25, 50, 100); part 2 o (0, 5, 10, 15, 17, 25, 50). Common values are 3, 10, 15, 20 (part 1) and 10, 15 (part 2). Round 4's table used Q3's budget-derived m and the nearest o at or below it.
6. Cheapest-valid-set rule: round 4's fixed rule has no `within_full`; round 5's `narrowest_valid` does. Which applies is a method choice.
7. Monte Carlo standard errors: F2 regime B keeps per-gene summaries only (`GSCHEMA`, `round10_final_runs_f2_regimeB.py` lines 37 to 42: coverage_se per gene, no per-draw widths), so a standard error of the width ratio needs per-draw output or a new run. W3's map carries `coverage_sd_folds` but no width error; the row-level `w3_o_sweep.csv.gz` (fold, draw) would allow a bootstrap over draws. Neither joint script computes a standard error.
